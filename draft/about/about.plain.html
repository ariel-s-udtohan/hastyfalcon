Our values

What we stand for

Radical transparency
Every tariff, every charge, every profit margin — we publish it all. You should know exactly what you're paying for and why.
Planet-first supply
We only buy from renewable generators. Not carbon-offset fossil fuels — actual wind, solar, and hydro power.
Long-term thinking
As an employee-owned business, we optimise for decades, not quarters. Our decisions reflect the world our staff will retire into.
